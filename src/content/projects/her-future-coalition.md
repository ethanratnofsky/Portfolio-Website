Her Future Coalition is a non-profit organization that actively works to fight
against human trafficking and gender violence in India. The organization
provides programs to help victims of these crimes and works to raise awareness
about these issues. Their programs and services provide beneficiaries with
education, job training, shelter, and mental health resources.

As an Engineering manager for Change++ at Vanderbilt University, I was
responsible for leading a team of 5 developers to create a web application for
Her Future Coalition. The web application allows the members of administration
within the organization to manage and analyze data describing the
organization's beneficiaries, programs, and workshops. Previously, the
organization was using paper and Excel spreadsheets to manage this data, which
was inefficient and prone to errors.
